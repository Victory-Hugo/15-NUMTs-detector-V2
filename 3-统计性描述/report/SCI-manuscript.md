# NUMTs Frequency Distribution Across Human Genomes: A Comprehensive Statistical Description
# 人类基因组中NUMTs频率分布：一项综合统计描述

## Abstract / 摘要

Nuclear mitochondrial DNA segments (NUMTs) represent mitochondrial DNA fragments that have integrated into the nuclear genome during evolution. While common NUMTs are shared across individuals and populations, rare and ultra-rare NUMTs capture recent integration events and population-specific variation. Here, we present a comprehensive statistical description of NUMT frequency distribution across a large cohort, characterizing 2,835 distinct NUMT loci in total, of which 2,244 (79.2%) are ultra-rare, 354 (12.5%) are rare, 119 (4.2%) are low-frequency, and only 118 (4.2%) are common. After excluding known reference NUMTs, 734 novel NUMT loci were identified, predominantly ultra-rare (93.7%). These findings reveal that the vast majority of NUMT variation exists at the rarest end of the frequency spectrum, with singleton events alone accounting for 48.9% of all detected loci.

核基因组中的线粒体DNA片段（NUMTs）代表在进化过程中整合到核基因组中的线粒体DNA碎片。虽然常见的NUMTs在个体和群体之间共享，但稀有和超稀有NUMTs捕获了最近的整合事件和群体特异性变异。在此，我们提出了一个大队列中NUMT频率分布的综合统计描述，共鉴定了2,835个不同的NUMT位点，其中2,244个（79.2%）为超稀有，354个（12.5%）为稀有，119个（4.2%）为低频，仅有118个（4.2%）为常见。排除已知参考NUMTs后，鉴定出734个新型NUMT位点，主要为超稀有（93.7%）。这些发现揭示了绝大多数NUMT变异存在于频率谱的最稀有端，仅单例事件就占所有检测位点的48.9%。

## Results / 结果

### Overview of NUMT Detection / NUMT检测概述

We systematically identified NUMT insertion events across our study cohort using a strict detection threshold. In total, 2,835 distinct NUMT loci were detected when considering all samples, including both public reference genomes and our newly sequenced cohort. These NUMTs spanned all twenty-four chromosomes, with the highest event counts on chromosome 1 (17,519 events), chromosome 2 (17,039 events), and chromosome 5 (8,211 events). Chromosomes 19, 18, and 22 harbored the fewest NUMT events, with 155, 798, and 895 events respectively. The chromosomal distribution of NUMT events broadly correlates with chromosome size, although certain chromosomes showed enrichment beyond what would be expected by length alone, suggesting non-random insertion preferences across the nuclear genome.

我们使用严格的检测阈值系统地鉴定了研究队列中的NUMT插入事件。在考虑所有样本（包括公共参考基因组和我们新测序的队列）的情况下，共检测到2,835个不同的NUMT位点。这些NUMTs覆盖了全部24条染色体，其中1号染色体（17,519个事件）、2号染色体（17,039个事件）和5号染色体（8,211个事件）的事件数最多。19号、18号和22号染色体的NUMT事件最少，分别为155个、798个和895个。NUMT事件的染色体分布与染色体大小大体相关，尽管某些染色体显示出超出仅由长度预期的富集，表明核基因组中存在非随机的插入偏好。

### NUMT Length Characteristics / NUMT长度特征

The detected NUMT regions exhibited substantial variation in length. Across all samples, the mean NUMT length was 350.4 base pairs, with a median of 111 base pairs, ranging from 29 to 16,568 base pairs. This marked discrepancy between the mean and median indicates a highly right-skewed distribution, where most NUMTs are short fragments while a minority of much longer insertions pull the mean upward. The longest NUMTs, exceeding ten kilobases, likely represent near-complete or complete mitochondrial genome insertions, whereas the short fragments below one hundred base pairs may correspond to degraded or fragmented integration events that have accumulated mutations and deletions over evolutionary time.

检测到的NUMT区域在长度上表现出显著的变异。在所有样本中，NUMT的平均长度为350.4个碱基对，中位数为111个碱基对，范围从29到16,568个碱基对。均值与中位数之间的显著差异表明这是一个高度右偏的分布，大多数NUMTs是短片段，而少数更长的插入片段将均值拉高。超过10千碱基的最长NUMTs可能代表接近完整或完整的线粒体基因组插入，而低于100个碱基对的短片段可能对应在进化过程中积累了突变和缺失的降解或碎片化整合事件。

### Frequency Spectrum / 频率谱

A central finding of this study is the extreme skew of NUMTs toward the rarest frequency classes. When NUMTs were classified by the number of samples carrying each locus, ultra-rare NUMTs constituted the overwhelming majority of detected loci. Among the 2,835 distinct NUMTs identified across all samples, ultra-rare NUMTs accounted for 2,244 loci (79.2%), rare NUMTs for 354 loci (12.5%), low-frequency NUMTs for 119 loci (4.2%), and common NUMTs for 118 loci (4.2%). This distribution demonstrates that the NUMT landscape is dominated by recently integrated, population-specific events. Common NUMTs, which represent ancient insertions shared across most individuals, account for the smallest proportion of distinct loci. The ratio of ultra-rare to common NUMTs exceeds nineteen to one, highlighting that NUMT polymorphism is an ongoing and highly individualized process.

本研究的一个核心发现是NUMTs极度偏向最稀有的频率类别。当按照携带每个位点的样本数对NUMTs进行分类时，超稀有NUMTs占检测位点的绝大多数。在所有样本中鉴定的2,835个不同NUMTs中，超稀有NUMTs占2,244个位点（79.2%），稀有NUMTs占354个位点（12.5%），低频NUMTs占119个位点（4.2%），常见NUMTs占118个位点（4.2%）。这一分布表明NUMT景观由最近整合的、群体特异性的事件主导。代表在大多数个体间共享的古老插入的常见NUMTs仅占不同位点的最小比例。超稀有与常见NUMTs的比率超过十九比一，凸显了NUMT多态性是一个持续的、高度个体化的过程。

### Singletons Dominate the Ultra-Rare Category / 单例主导超稀有类别

Within the ultra-rare category, singleton NUMTs observed in only one individual were particularly abundant. At a minimum support threshold of one sample, we detected 1,385 singleton clusters, representing 48.9% of all distinct NUMT loci. Doubleton clusters, present in exactly two samples, accounted for 371 loci (13.1%), and tripleton clusters, present in exactly three samples, for 170 loci (6.0%). Together, singletons, doubletons, and tripletons comprised 68.0% of all detected NUMTs, underscoring that most NUMT insertions are recent events that have not yet spread through the population. As the minimum sample support threshold increased, the number of retained NUMTs declined steeply. Specifically, 1,450 NUMTs were present in at least two samples (51.1%), 1,079 in at least three (38.1%), 810 in at least five (28.6%), 591 in at least ten (20.8%), 304 in at least fifty (10.7%), and only 237 in at least one hundred samples (8.4%). This rapid decay in NUMT count with increasing support threshold is consistent with a model in which most NUMT integrations are recent and have not had time to reach high population frequencies.

在超稀有类别中，仅在一个个体中观察到的单例NUMTs特别丰富。在最小样本支持阈值为1的情况下，我们检测到1,385个单例簇，占所有不同NUMT位点的48.9%。恰好在两个样本中存在的双例簇占371个位点（13.1%），恰好在三个样本中存在的三例簇占170个位点（6.0%）。单例、双例和三例合计占所有检测NUMTs的68.0%，强调了大多数NUMT插入是尚未在群体中传播的近期事件。随着最小样本支持阈值的增加，保留的NUMTs数量急剧下降。具体而言，1,450个NUMTs存在于至少两个样本中（51.1%），1,079个存在于至少三个样本中（38.1%），810个存在于至少五个样本中（28.6%），591个存在于至少十个样本中（20.8%），304个存在于至少五十个样本中（10.7%），仅有237个存在于至少一百个样本中（8.4%）。NUMT数量随支持阈值增加而快速衰减，与大多数NUMT整合是近期的、尚未来得及达到高群体频率的模型一致。

### Relative Frequency Analysis / 相对频率分析

The mean frequency of common NUMTs was 0.211, meaning these ancient insertions were present in approximately 21.1% of all samples, establishing a baseline for widely shared events. Low-frequency NUMTs had a mean frequency of 0.022, representing approximately 10.5% of the common NUMT frequency. Rare NUMTs averaged 0.003, or 1.5% of the common frequency, while ultra-rare NUMTs averaged 0.0002, merely 0.096% of the common NUMT frequency. Singleton NUMTs, the rarest subset, had a mean frequency of 0.0001, representing only 0.048% of the common NUMT mean frequency. These relative frequency measurements confirm that the NUMT frequency spectrum follows an approximately log-linear decay from common to ultra-rare categories, consistent with neutral or nearly neutral population genetic models for recent insertions.

常见NUMTs的平均频率为0.211，意味着这些古老插入存在于约21.1%的所有样本中，为广泛共享的事件建立了基线。低频NUMTs的平均频率为0.022，约为常见NUMT频率的10.5%。稀有NUMTs平均为0.003，即常见频率的1.5%，而超稀有NUMTs平均为0.0002，仅为常见NUMT频率的0.096%。单例NUMTs作为最稀有的子集，平均频率为0.0001，仅占常见NUMT平均频率的0.048%。这些相对频率测量结果证实，NUMT频率谱从常见到超稀有类别遵循近似对数线性衰减，与近期插入的中性或近中性群体遗传学模型一致。

### Novel NUMTs After Excluding Reference Variants / 排除参考变异后的新型NUMTs

To identify NUMTs that are specific to our study cohort and not present in existing reference databases, we excluded all known reference NUMTs and re-analyzed the frequency distribution. After this filtering, 734 distinct NUMT loci remained across all samples. The frequency spectrum of these novel NUMTs was even more skewed toward the rare end than the full dataset. Ultra-rare NUMTs dominated with 688 loci (93.7%), followed by rare NUMTs with 38 loci (5.2%), low-frequency NUMTs with 8 loci (1.1%), and notably, zero common NUMTs. The complete absence of common NUMTs among novel loci is expected, as any NUMT that is truly common across human populations would likely have already been catalogued in reference databases. The remaining novel NUMTs are almost exclusively ultra-rare, consistent with the hypothesis that these represent very recent, population- or family-specific integration events. The mean length of novel NUMTs was 74.6 base pairs with a median of 67.0 base pairs, substantially shorter than the overall mean of 350.4 base pairs, suggesting that newer NUMT insertions tend to be more fragmented. Among samples present in at least two individuals, only 194 novel NUMTs remained, and this number dropped to 38 at a support threshold of ten samples. Only seven novel NUMTs were found in at least one hundred samples, representing potential candidates for previously unannotated ancient NUMT insertions.

为了鉴定我们研究队列特有的、不存在于现有参考数据库中的NUMTs，我们排除了所有已知参考NUMTs并重新分析了频率分布。经过此过滤后，所有样本中剩余734个不同的NUMT位点。这些新型NUMTs的频率谱比完整数据集更加偏向稀有端。超稀有NUMTs占主导，有688个位点（93.7%），其次是稀有NUMTs 38个位点（5.2%），低频NUMTs 8个位点（1.1%），值得注意的是，常见NUMTs为零。新型位点中常见NUMTs的完全缺失是可以预期的，因为任何真正跨越人类群体常见的NUMTs可能已经被收录在参考数据库中。剩余的新型NUMTs几乎完全是超稀有的，与这些代表非常近期的、群体或家族特异性整合事件的假设一致。新型NUMTs的平均长度为74.6个碱基对，中位数为67.0个碱基对，远短于总体平均值350.4个碱基对，表明较新的NUMT插入倾向于更加碎片化。在至少两个个体中存在的样本中，仅剩余194个新型NUMTs，在十个样本的支持阈值下该数字降至38个。仅有7个新型NUMTs在至少一百个样本中被发现，代表了先前未注释的古老NUMT插入的潜在候选者。

### New Cohort Samples / 新队列样本

When analyzing only the newly sequenced samples in our cohort, excluding public reference genomes, we identified 1,098 distinct NUMT loci. The frequency distribution within this subset mirrored the pattern observed in the full dataset. Ultra-rare NUMTs accounted for 966 loci (88.0%), rare NUMTs for 94 loci (8.6%), low-frequency NUMTs for 22 loci (2.0%), and common NUMTs for 16 loci (1.5%). The mean NUMT length in the new cohort samples was 334.7 base pairs with a median of 118 base pairs, similar to the overall cohort. Singleton NUMTs accounted for 666 loci (60.7% of all NUMTs in the new samples), and together with doubletons (141 loci, 12.8%) and tripletons (61 loci, 5.6%), these low-frequency events comprised 79.1% of all detected NUMTs in the new cohort. After excluding reference NUMTs within the new cohort, 2,273 distinct loci were identified. Among these reference-excluded NUMTs, ultra-rare variants predominated with 1,812 loci (79.7%), accompanied by 265 rare (11.7%), 97 low-frequency (4.3%), and 99 common (4.4%) loci. The mean length of these reference-excluded NUMTs was 93.8 base pairs with a median of 90.0 base pairs, again shorter than the overall average.

当仅分析队列中新测序的样本（排除公共参考基因组）时，我们鉴定了1,098个不同的NUMT位点。该子集中的频率分布反映了在完整数据集中观察到的模式。超稀有NUMTs占966个位点（88.0%），稀有NUMTs占94个位点（8.6%），低频NUMTs占22个位点（2.0%），常见NUMTs占16个位点（1.5%）。新队列样本中NUMT的平均长度为334.7个碱基对，中位数为118个碱基对，与总体队列相似。单例NUMTs占666个位点（新样本中所有NUMTs的60.7%），与双例（141个位点，12.8%）和三例（61个位点，5.6%）一起，这些低频事件占新队列中所有检测NUMTs的79.1%。在新队列中排除参考NUMTs后，鉴定了2,273个不同的位点。在这些排除参考的NUMTs中，超稀有变异占主导，有1,812个位点（79.7%），伴有265个稀有（11.7%）、97个低频（4.3%）和99个常见（4.4%）位点。这些排除参考的NUMTs的平均长度为93.8个碱基对，中位数为90.0个碱基对，再次短于总体平均值。

### Highly Recurrent NUMT Clusters / 高度复发的NUMT簇

Among the most frequently observed NUMT clusters, several loci were detected in the vast majority of samples. The top recurrent NUMT on chromosome 17, with a midpoint at approximately 22.53 megabases, was present in 9,822 samples, representing a nearly fixed insertion in the human population with a frequency of 99.99%. Other highly recurrent NUMTs included loci on chromosome 1 at approximately 2.57 megabases, present in 80.9% of samples, chromosome 11 at approximately 49.86 megabases in 69.0%, chromosome 5 at approximately 32.34 megabases in 62.3%, and chromosome 2 at approximately 32.92 megabases in 60.9%. These common NUMTs likely represent ancient integration events that occurred before the diversification of modern human populations and have been maintained in the gene pool across evolutionary time.

在最常观察到的NUMT簇中，几个位点在绝大多数样本中被检测到。17号染色体上最顶级的复发NUMTs，中点约为22.53兆碱基，存在于9,822个样本中，代表了人类群体中几乎固定的插入，频率为99.99%。其他高度复发的NUMTs包括1号染色体上约2.57兆碱基的位点，存在于80.9%的样本中，11号染色体上约49.86兆碱基的位点存在于69.0%的样本中，5号染色体上约32.34兆碱基的位点存在于62.3%的样本中，以及2号染色体上约32.92兆碱基的位点存在于60.9%的样本中。这些常见的NUMTs可能代表了在现代人类群体分化之前发生的古老整合事件，并在进化过程中在基因库中得以维持。

## Discussion / 讨论

Our comprehensive analysis of NUMT frequency distribution across a large human cohort reveals several key patterns. The NUMT landscape is overwhelmingly dominated by ultra-rare variants, with nearly 80% of all detected NUMT loci falling into this category. This finding is consistent with a model of continuous, low-rate NUMT integration during human evolution, where most insertion events are recent and have not spread widely through populations. The near-complete absence of common NUMTs among novel loci supports the completeness of existing NUMT reference databases for ancient, population-shared insertions. The novel NUMTs we identified are almost exclusively ultra-rare, suggesting they represent very recent integration events that are specific to particular populations, families, or even individuals.

我们对大人类队列中NUMT频率分布的综合分析揭示了几个关键模式。NUMT景观由超稀有变异压倒性地主导，几乎所有检测到的NUMT位点中近80%属于这一类别。这一发现与人类进化过程中持续、低速率NUMT整合的模型一致，其中大多数插入事件是近期的，尚未在群体中广泛传播。新型位点中常见NUMTs的几乎完全缺失支持了现有NUMT参考数据库对于古老的、群体共享的插入的完整性。我们鉴定的新型NUMTs几乎完全是超稀有的，表明它们代表了特定群体、家族甚至个体所特有的非常近期的整合事件。

The length distribution analysis provides additional insights into NUMT evolutionary dynamics. Novel NUMTs are substantially shorter than the overall average, which may reflect either a change in the mechanism of NUMT integration over time or the progressive degradation of older NUMT sequences. The shorter length of recent insertions could also indicate that the current generation of NUMTs arises from fragmented mitochondrial DNA rather than complete organelle genome capture. The extreme enrichment of singleton NUMTs, accounting for 48.9% of all loci, has important implications for population genetics and medical genomics. These individual-specific NUMTs contribute to personal genomic variation and may have functional consequences if they disrupt coding or regulatory regions. Their rarity also means they are largely invisible to standard variant databases, highlighting the importance of large-scale, deep-coverage sequencing efforts for comprehensive NUMT characterization.

长度分布分析为NUMT进化动态提供了额外的见解。新型NUMTs明显短于总体平均值，这可能反映了NUMT整合机制随时间的变化，或较旧NUMT序列的逐渐降解。较近插入的较短长度也可能表明当前一代的NUMTs源于碎片化的线粒体DNA，而非完整的细胞器基因组捕获。单例NUMTs的极端富集，占所有位点的48.9%，对群体遗传学和医学基因组学具有重要意义。这些个体特异性的NUMTs贡献了个人基因组变异，如果它们破坏了编码或调控区域，可能会产生功能后果。它们的稀有性也意味着它们在标准变异数据库中基本不可见，凸显了大规模、深度覆盖测序工作对于全面NUMT特征描述的重要性。

## Methods / 方法

### NUMT Detection and Filtering / NUMT检测与过滤

NUMT insertion events were detected using a strict threshold-based pipeline applied to whole-genome sequencing data. The pipeline identifies mitochondrial DNA insertions into the nuclear genome through discordant read-pair and split-read analysis. Only events meeting stringent quality criteria were retained for downstream analysis. The frequency classification system categorized NUMTs into four groups based on the number of samples carrying each locus: common, low-frequency, rare, and ultra-rare. Additionally, NUMTs were categorized as singleton, doubleton, or tripleton based on their exact sample count. To identify novel NUMTs specific to our study cohort, all known reference NUMTs from existing databases were excluded from the analysis, removing ancient, well-characterized NUMT insertions and retaining only population-specific or individual-specific events. NUMT length statistics were computed across all detected regions, chromosomal event counts were tallied per chromosome, and relative frequency percentages were calculated by comparing the mean frequency of each category to the common NUMT mean frequency, establishing the common category as the one hundred percent reference baseline.

NUMT插入事件使用应用于全基因组测序数据的严格阈值检测流程进行检测。该流程通过不一致读段对和拆分读段分析鉴定线粒体DNA向核基因组的插入。仅保留满足严格质量标准的事件用于下游分析。频率分类系统根据携带每个位点的样本数将NUMTs分为四组：常见、低频、稀有和超稀有。此外，NUMTs根据其精确样本数被分类为单例、双例或三例。为了鉴定我们研究队列特有的新型NUMTs，从分析中排除了现有数据库中所有已知参考NUMTs，去除了古老的、特征明确的NUMT插入，仅保留群体特异性或个体特异性事件。NUMT长度统计在所有检测区域上计算，染色体事件计数按染色体统计，相对频率百分比通过将每个类别的平均频率与常见NUMT平均频率进行比较来计算，将常见类别设定为百分之百的参考基线。
