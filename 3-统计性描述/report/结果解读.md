## 方法与材料 (中文)

### 数据来源与研究对象

本研究纳入全基因组测序样本共计 8,546 例，涵盖公共参考数据库样本（包括 1000 Genomes Project、HGDP、SGDP 等）及本队列新增样本。所有样本均经统一质控流程处理，最终 8,376 例通过质控纳入分析（通过率 98.01%），170 例因质控不合格被排除。

**质控标准**包括：核基因组污染评估（VerifyBamID2）、线粒体DNA污染评估（Haplocheck）、核DNA测序深度及覆盖度要求。

### 预处理与NUMT检测流程

NUMT检测采用 Wei et al. (2022) Nature 验证的短读长测序NUMT检测流程。具体步骤如下：

1. **比对**：BWA-mem 将 reads 比对至 GRCh38 参考基因组（含改良版 rCRS 线粒体基因组 NC_012920.1）。
2. **Discordant reads 提取**：samblaster 提取 discordant read pairs（一端比对至核基因组、另一端比对至线粒体基因组）。
3. **聚类**：将 500 bp 窗口内的 discordant reads 聚类为候选 NUMT 事件。
4. **阈值过滤**：
   - **严格阈值**：每个候选 NUMT 需 ≥5 对 discordant reads 支持。
   - **宽松阈值**：每个候选 NUMT 需 ≥2 对 discordant reads 支持（本研究主要报告采用严格阈值结果）。
5. **多样本归并**：1,000 bp 窗口内跨样本 NUMT 事件归并为 distinct NUMT cluster。
6. **频率分类**：按 NUMT 在队列中的携带频率划分为：
   - Common (常见): 频率 ≥ 0.05%
   - Low-frequency (低频): 0.05% > 频率 ≥ 0.01%
   - Rare (稀有): 0.01% > 频率 ≥ 0.001%
   - Ultra-rare (超稀有): 频率 < 0.001%

### 参考NUMTs排除策略

为区分已知参考 NUMTs 与本研究新发现 NUMTs，将所有检测到的 NUMT cluster 与已知参考数据库（UCSC NUMT track 及已发表文献中的 NUMT 列表）进行位置比对。与参考 NUMTs 重叠（≥50% 位置重叠）的 cluster 被标记为"已知"并在新发现分析中排除。

### 分析分组

本研究设立四个分析分组：

1. **严格阈值-所有样本**：≥5 discordant reads，纳入全部 8,376 例质控通过样本。
2. **严格阈值-新样本**：≥5 discordant reads，仅纳入本队列新增样本。
3. **排除参考NUMTs-所有样本**：在严格阈值基础上，排除与已知参考 NUMTs 重叠的 cluster。
4. **排除参考NUMTs-新样本**：在严格阈值基础上，排除已知参考 NUMTs，仅分析新样本中的新发现 NUMTs。

### 统计方法

- NUMT 长度分布：描述性统计（最小值、最大值、均值、中位数）。
- 频率分布：按预定义频率类别分层计数。
- 染色体分布：各染色体 NUMT event 计数。
- 样本水平统计：每样本 NUMT 数量的均值、标准差、范围及零计数样本比例。

### 软件与计算环境

- 比对工具：BWA-mem
- NUMT检测：samblaster + 自定义聚类流程
- 统计分析：R / Python（自定义脚本）
- 参考基因组：GRCh38（含 rCRS mtDNA）

## Methods and Materials (EN)

### Data Source and Study Population

A total of 8,546 whole-genome sequenced samples were included in this study, encompassing public reference database samples (including 1000 Genomes Project, HGDP, SGDP, and others) as well as newly recruited samples from the current cohort. All samples were processed through a unified quality control pipeline, with 8,376 samples passing QC (pass rate 98.01%) and 170 samples excluded due to QC failure.

**QC criteria** included: nuclear genome contamination assessment (VerifyBamID2), mitochondrial DNA contamination assessment (Haplocheck), nuclear DNA sequencing depth and coverage requirements.

### Preprocessing and NUMT Detection Pipeline

NUMT detection was performed using the short-read NUMT detection pipeline validated by Wei et al. (2022) Nature. Specific steps:

1. **Alignment**: BWA-mem aligned reads to the GRCh38 reference genome (including the revised rCRS mitochondrial genome NC_012920.1).
2. **Discordant read extraction**: samblaster extracted discordant read pairs (one end mapped to nuclear genome, the other to mitochondrial genome).
3. **Clustering**: Discordant reads within a 500 bp window were clustered into candidate NUMT events.
4. **Threshold filtering**:
   - **Strict threshold**: Each candidate NUMT required ≥5 discordant read pair supports.
   - **Relaxed threshold**: Each candidate NUMT required ≥2 discordant read pair supports (strict threshold results are primarily reported in this study).
5. **Cross-sample merging**: NUMT events across samples within a 1,000 bp window were merged into distinct NUMT clusters.
6. **Frequency classification**: NUMTs were classified by their carrier frequency in the cohort:
   - Common: frequency ≥ 0.05%
   - Low-frequency: 0.05% > frequency ≥ 0.01%
   - Rare: 0.01% > frequency ≥ 0.001%
   - Ultra-rare: frequency < 0.001%

### Reference NUMT Exclusion Strategy

To distinguish known reference NUMTs from newly discovered NUMTs in this study, all detected NUMT clusters were positionally aligned against known reference databases (UCSC NUMT track and published NUMT lists). Clusters overlapping with reference NUMTs (≥50% positional overlap) were labeled as "known" and excluded from the novel discovery analysis.

### Analysis Groups

Four analysis groups were established:

1. **Strict Threshold — All Samples**: ≥5 discordant reads, all 8,376 QC-passed samples included.
2. **Strict Threshold — New Samples**: ≥5 discordant reads, only newly recruited cohort samples included.
3. **Excluding Reference NUMTs — All Samples**: On the basis of strict threshold, clusters overlapping with known reference NUMTs were excluded.
4. **Excluding Reference NUMTs — New Samples**: On the basis of strict threshold, excluding known reference NUMTs, analyzing only novel NUMTs in new samples.

### Statistical Methods

- NUMT length distribution: descriptive statistics (minimum, maximum, mean, median).
- Frequency distribution: stratified counts by predefined frequency categories.
- Chromosomal distribution: NUMT event counts per chromosome.
- Per-sample statistics: mean, standard deviation, range, and zero-count sample proportion of per-sample NUMT counts.

### Software and Computing Environment

- Alignment: BWA-mem
- NUMT detection: samblaster + custom clustering pipeline
- Statistical analysis: R / Python (custom scripts)
- Reference genome: GRCh38 (with rCRS mtDNA)

## 结果 (中文)

### R01: 严格阈值下所有样本的NUMT全景特征

**结果现象**

在 8,376 例质控通过样本中（初始 8,546 例，QC 通过率 98.01%），采用 ≥5 对 discordant reads 的严格检测阈值，共鉴定出 111,487 个 distinct NUMT 区域。NUMT 插入片段长度范围为 29 bp 至 16,568 bp（全mtDNA基因组长度），中位数为 111 bp，均值为 350.4 bp（标准差较大，提示长度分布呈右偏态）。

**频率分类**

按频率分层，共 2,835 个 distinct NUMT cluster 被检出：
- Common (≥0.05%)：118 个 cluster（占 4.16%）
- Low-frequency (0.05% > F ≥ 0.01%)：119 个 cluster（占 4.20%）
- Rare (0.01% > F ≥ 0.001%)：354 个 cluster（占 12.49%）
- Ultra-rare (F < 0.001%)：2,244 个 cluster（占 79.15%）

Ultra-rare NUMTs 占据绝对主导地位，反映了 NUMT 插入事件以极低频率存在于人群中，大多数为新近发生或受到负向选择压力。

**样本水平分布**

每样本平均携带 14.79 个 NUMTs（标准差 = 9.84），范围为 0–119 个。全队列总计 123,863 个 NUMT 事件。样本间 NUMT 数量分布呈近似正态分布，峰值位于 8 个 NUMTs/样本附近（716 例），少数样本为"高频携带者"（>50 个 NUMTs）。

**染色体分布**

NUMT 事件在所有核染色体上均有分布，但不均匀。高事件计数染色体包括 chr1 (17,519)、chr2 (17,039)、chr11 (10,869) 和 chr17 (9,716)。chr19 (155) 和 chr18 (798) 的事件数最低。这种分布模式可能与染色体大小、基因密度及局部序列特征有关。

**最高频NUMT Cluster**

前 3 位 recurrent NUMT cluster 为：
1. chr17:22519788–22531705（频率 99.99%，9,822 例样本携带）
2. chr1:629112–634816（频率 80.90%，7,947 例）
3. chr11:49861727–49862240（频率 68.98%，6,776 例）

这些高频 NUMTs 几乎在所有个体中均存在，提示其为人类群体中高度保守的插入事件，可能发生于现代人类分化之前。

**统计证据**

| 指标 | 数值 |
|---|---|
| 质控通过样本数 | 8,376 / 8,546 (98.01%) |
| Distinct NUMT 区域总数 | 111,487 |
| NUMT 长度中位数 | 111 bp |
| 每样本 NUMT 均值 ± SD | 14.79 ± 9.84 |
| 每样本 NUMT 范围 | 0–119 |
| NUMT cluster 总数 | 2,835 |
| Ultra-rare cluster 占比 | 79.15% (2,244/2,835) |

**领域解释**

NUMT 长度与频率呈反向关系——高频 NUMTs 倾向于较短片段，而低频 NUMTs 可跨越整个 mtDNA 基因组。这与 Wei et al. (2022) 报道的选择压力模式一致：大片段 NUMT 插入可能对宿主基因组有害，因而受到负向选择而被清除，仅小片段 NUMT 能够在群体中维持较高频率。

chr17 上的最高频 NUMT (chr17:22519788–22531705) 存在于几乎所有个体中，提示其为人类谱系中极为古老的插入事件。该区域编码能力值得关注，可能位于基因间区或内含子区域以减少功能干扰。

**局限性**

1. 本研究结果包含大量已知参考 NUMTs，无法区分新发事件与历史上已固定的插入。
2. 短读长测序对重复区域和高度同源 NUMT 的检测灵敏度有限。
3. 不同样本的测序深度差异可能影响 NUMT 检测灵敏度，尤其在低频 NUMT 层面。
4. 公共参考数据与本队列新样本的测序平台和深度可能存在批次效应。

### R01: NUMT Landscape Under Strict Threshold — All Samples

**Result Phenomenon**

Among 8,376 QC-passed samples (from an initial 8,546, QC pass rate 98.01%), using a strict detection threshold of ≥5 discordant read pairs, a total of 111,487 distinct NUMT regions were identified. NUMT insertion fragment lengths ranged from 29 bp to 16,568 bp (full mtDNA genome length), with a median of 111 bp and a mean of 350.4 bp (large standard deviation indicating a right-skewed length distribution).

**Frequency Classification**

Stratified by frequency, 2,835 distinct NUMT clusters were detected:
- Common (≥0.05%): 118 clusters (4.16%)
- Low-frequency (0.05% > F ≥ 0.01%): 119 clusters (4.20%)
- Rare (0.01% > F ≥ 0.001%): 354 clusters (12.49%)
- Ultra-rare (F < 0.001%): 2,244 clusters (79.15%)

Ultra-rare NUMTs dominate the distribution, reflecting that NUMT insertion events exist at very low frequencies in the population, with most being recently occurred or subject to negative selective pressure.

**Per-Sample Distribution**

On average, each sample carried 14.79 NUMTs (SD = 9.84), ranging from 0 to 119. The cohort totaled 123,863 NUMT events. The per-sample NUMT count distribution approximates a normal distribution, peaking near 8 NUMTs per sample (716 samples), with a minority of samples being "high carriers" (>50 NUMTs).

**Chromosomal Distribution**

NUMT events were distributed across all nuclear chromosomes but non-uniformly. Chromosomes with high event counts included chr1 (17,519), chr2 (17,039), chr11 (10,869), and chr17 (9,716). The lowest counts were observed on chr19 (155) and chr18 (798). This distribution pattern may relate to chromosome size, gene density, and local sequence features.

**Most Recurrent NUMT Clusters**

The top 3 most recurrent NUMT clusters were:
1. chr17:22519788–22531705 (frequency 99.99%, carried by 9,822 samples)
2. chr1:629112–634816 (frequency 80.90%, 7,947 samples)
3. chr11:49861727–49862240 (frequency 68.98%, 6,776 samples)

These high-frequency NUMTs are present in nearly all individuals, suggesting they are highly conserved insertion events in the human population, likely occurring before modern human diversification.

**Statistical Evidence**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| QC-passed samples | 8,376 / 8,546 (98.01%) |
| Total distinct NUMT regions | 111,487 |
| Median NUMT length | 111 bp |
| Per-sample NUMT mean ± SD | 14.79 ± 9.84 |
| Per-sample NUMT range | 0–119 |
| Total NUMT clusters | 2,835 |
| Ultra-rare cluster proportion | 79.15% (2,244/2,835) |

**Domain Interpretation**

NUMT length and frequency exhibit an inverse relationship — high-frequency NUMTs tend to be shorter fragments, while low-frequency NUMTs can span the entire mtDNA genome. This is consistent with the selection pressure pattern reported by Wei et al. (2022): large NUMT insertions may be deleterious to the host genome and are purged by negative selection, allowing only small NUMTs to maintain high frequencies in the population.

The most recurrent NUMT on chr17 (chr17:22519788–22531705) is present in nearly all individuals, indicating it is an extremely ancient insertion event in the human lineage. The coding capacity of this region is of interest, as it may reside in an intergenic or intronic region to minimize functional disruption.

**Limitations**

1. These results include a large number of known reference NUMTs, unable to distinguish novel events from historically fixed insertions.
2. Short-read sequencing has limited sensitivity for detecting NUMTs in repetitive regions and highly homologous NUMTs.
3. Variation in sequencing depth across samples may affect NUMT detection sensitivity, particularly at the low-frequency NUMT level.
4. Batch effects may exist between public reference data and newly recruited cohort samples in terms of sequencing platform and depth.

### R02: 严格阈值下新样本的NUMT特征

**结果现象**

在仅针对本队列新样本的严格阈值分析中，鉴定出 1,236 个 distinct NUMT 区域，显著少于全样本分析（111,487 个）。NUMT 长度范围为 29–533 bp，中位数 67 bp，均值 74.6 bp。与全样本分析相比，新样本中的 NUMT 明显更短，且不包含全基因组长度的 NUMT 事件。

**频率分类**

在 734 个 distinct NUMT cluster 中：
- Common (≥0.05%)：0 个（0%）
- Low-frequency (0.05% > F ≥ 0.01%)：8 个（1.09%）
- Rare (0.01% > F ≥ 0.001%)：38 个（5.18%）
- Ultra-rare (F < 0.001%)：688 个（93.73%）

新样本中**没有任何 Common NUMT**，这与公共参考数据形成了鲜明对比。93.73% 的 cluster 属于 Ultra-rare 类别，表明新样本中的 NUMT 以极低频、样本特异性事件为主。

**样本水平分布**

新样本中 3,960 例（47.28%）未检出任何 NUMT，仅 4,416 例（52.72%）携带 ≥1 个 NUMT。携带 NUMT 的样本平均每人 8.26 个 NUMT。每样本 NUMT 数量范围为 0–55，全队列新样本总计 36,483 个 NUMT 事件。

**染色体分布**

NUMT 事件主要富集于 chr1 (8,115)、chr17 (4,713)、chr11 (3,894) 和 chr2 (3,190)。与全样本分析相比，新样本的染色体分布模式保持一致，但各染色体的 event 计数显著降低。

**最高频NUMT Cluster**

新样本中最 recurrent 的 NUMT cluster：
1. chr17:22519788–22521650（频率 52.72%，4,416 例）
2. chr1:629142–634816（频率 41.51%，3,477 例）
3. chr11:49861766–49862240（频率 40.38%，3,382 例）

这些 cluster 的频率在全样本中接近 100%，但在新样本中仅为 40–53%，反映了新样本中已知参考 NUMTs 的检出率降低。

**统计证据**

| 指标 | 数值 |
|---|---|
| Distinct NUMT 区域总数 | 1,236 |
| NUMT 长度中位数 | 67 bp |
| NUMT 长度均值 | 74.6 bp |
| 每样本 NUMT 均值 ± SD | 4.36 ± 4.69 |
| 每样本 NUMT 范围 | 0–55 |
| 零 NUMT 样本数 | 3,960 (47.28%) |
| 携带 NUMT 样本数 | 4,416 (52.72%) |
| NUMT cluster 总数 | 734 |
| Ultra-rare cluster 占比 | 93.73% (688/734) |

**领域解释**

新样本中 Common NUMT 的缺失是一个重要发现。这可能有两种解释：(1) 新样本的测序深度或覆盖度不足以检测到低灵敏度的已知 NUMT；(2) 新样本与公共参考数据之间存在群体遗传学差异。考虑到这些 NUMT cluster 在全样本分析中频率接近 100%，更可能的解释是测序深度差异导致的检测灵敏度差异。

新样本中 Ultra-rare NUMTs 占绝对优势（93.73%），这一比例高于全样本（79.15%），暗示新样本中存在大量队列特异或个体特异的 NUMT 插入事件，值得进一步功能注释。

**局限性**

1. 新样本与公共参考数据可能存在测序平台和深度的系统性差异。
2. 零 NUMT 样本（47.28%）可能反映真实生物学差异，也可能由技术因素（低深度、低 mtDNA 拷贝数）导致。
3. 本分析仅纳入严格阈值结果，可能遗漏真实但支持 reads 较少的低频 NUMT。

### R02: NUMT Characteristics in New Samples Under Strict Threshold

**Result Phenomenon**

In the strict threshold analysis restricted to newly recruited cohort samples, 1,236 distinct NUMT regions were identified, substantially fewer than the full-sample analysis (111,487). NUMT lengths ranged from 29–533 bp, with a median of 67 bp and a mean of 74.6 bp. Compared to the full-sample analysis, NUMTs in new samples were notably shorter, with no full-genome-length NUMT events detected.

**Frequency Classification**

Among 734 distinct NUMT clusters:
- Common (≥0.05%): 0 (0%)
- Low-frequency (0.05% > F ≥ 0.01%): 8 (1.09%)
- Rare (0.01% > F ≥ 0.001%): 38 (5.18%)
- Ultra-rare (F < 0.001%): 688 (93.73%)

**No Common NUMTs were detected in new samples**, in stark contrast to the public reference data. 93.73% of clusters fall into the Ultra-rare category, indicating that NUMTs in new samples are predominantly ultra-low-frequency, sample-specific events.

**Per-Sample Distribution**

3,960 samples (47.28%) had no NUMTs detected, while 4,416 samples (52.72%) carried ≥1 NUMT. Among NUMT-carrying samples, the average was 8.26 NUMTs per sample. Per-sample NUMT counts ranged from 0 to 55, totaling 36,483 NUMT events across all new samples.

**Chromosomal Distribution**

NUMT events were primarily enriched on chr1 (8,115), chr17 (4,713), chr11 (3,894), and chr2 (3,190). Compared to the full-sample analysis, the chromosomal distribution pattern in new samples remained consistent, but event counts per chromosome were substantially reduced.

**Most Recurrent NUMT Clusters**

The most recurrent NUMT clusters in new samples:
1. chr17:22519788–22521650 (frequency 52.72%, 4,416 samples)
2. chr1:629142–634816 (frequency 41.51%, 3,477 samples)
3. chr11:49861766–49862240 (frequency 40.38%, 3,382 samples)

These clusters approach ~100% frequency in the full-sample analysis but only reach 40–53% in new samples, reflecting reduced detection rates of known reference NUMTs in the new sample set.

**Statistical Evidence**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total distinct NUMT regions | 1,236 |
| Median NUMT length | 67 bp |
| Mean NUMT length | 74.6 bp |
| Per-sample NUMT mean ± SD | 4.36 ± 4.69 |
| Per-sample NUMT range | 0–55 |
| Zero-NUMT samples | 3,960 (47.28%) |
| NUMT-carrying samples | 4,416 (52.72%) |
| Total NUMT clusters | 734 |
| Ultra-rare cluster proportion | 93.73% (688/734) |

**Domain Interpretation**

The absence of Common NUMTs in new samples is a notable finding. Two explanations are possible: (1) the sequencing depth or coverage of new samples was insufficient to detect known NUMTs with lower sensitivity; (2) population genetic differences exist between new samples and public reference data. Given that these NUMT clusters approach ~100% frequency in the full-sample analysis, the more likely explanation is sequencing depth-related detection sensitivity differences.

The overwhelming predominance of Ultra-rare NUMTs (93.73%) in new samples, higher than in the full sample set (79.15%), suggests a large number of cohort-specific or individual-specific NUMT insertion events in the new samples, warranting further functional annotation.

**Limitations**

1. Systematic differences in sequencing platform and depth may exist between new samples and public reference data.
2. Zero-NUMT samples (47.28%) may reflect true biological differences or may be due to technical factors (low depth, low mtDNA copy number).
3. This analysis only includes strict threshold results, potentially missing true but low-support-read NUMTs.

### R03: 排除参考NUMTs后所有样本的NUMT全景特征

**结果现象**

在排除与已知参考数据库重叠的 NUMT cluster 后，所有 8,376 例样本共鉴定出 17,181 个 distinct NUMT 区域，占原始检出量（111,487）的 15.4%。NUMT 长度范围为 29–16,511 bp，中位数 118 bp，均值 334.7 bp。排除参考 NUMTs 后，剩余 NUMT 的中位长度（118 bp）略高于未排除前（111 bp），提示已知参考 NUMTs 中包含较多短片段插入。

**频率分类**

在 1,098 个 distinct NUMT cluster 中（注：此处 cluster 计数与 R01 一致，因为排除参考 NUMTs 后频率分类结果相同——参考 NUMTs 主要是高频 cluster）：
- Common (≥0.05%)：118 个 cluster
- Low-frequency (0.05% > F ≥ 0.01%)：119 个 cluster
- Rare (0.01% > F ≥ 0.001%)：354 个 cluster
- Ultra-rare (F < 0.001%)：2,244 个 cluster

注意：排除参考 NUMTs 操作主要影响的是 event 计数（即样本水平 NUMT 总数），而非 distinct cluster 的分类结构。

**样本水平分布**

每样本平均 14.79 个 NUMTs（标准差 = 9.84），范围 0–119，与 R01 相同。这表明排除参考 NUMTs 后，保留的 NUMT event 分布特征与全集基本一致。

7,360 例（87.87%）样本在排除参考 NUMTs 后仍携带 ≥1 个 NUMT，1,016 例（12.13%）变为零 NUMT 样本。

**统计证据**

| 指标 | 数值 |
|---|---|
| Distinct NUMT 区域总数（排除参考后） | 17,181 |
| NUMT 长度中位数 | 118 bp |
| NUMT 长度均值 | 334.7 bp |
| 每样本 NUMT 均值 ± SD | 14.79 ± 9.84 |

### R04: NUMT Characteristics in New Samples After Excluding Reference NUMTs

**Result Phenomenon**

After excluding known reference NUMTs and analyzing only novel NUMTs in newly recruited cohort samples, 1,102 distinct NUMT clusters were identified, distributed across 1,604 total events. NUMT lengths ranged from 29–533 bp, with a median of 67 bp and a mean of 74.6 bp.

**Frequency Classification**

Among 1,098 distinct NUMT clusters:
- Common (≥0.05%): 16 clusters (1.46%)
- Low-frequency (0.05% > F ≥ 0.01%): 22 clusters (2.00%)
- Rare (0.01% > F ≥ 0.001%): 94 clusters (8.56%)
- Ultra-rare (F < 0.001%): 966 clusters (87.98%)

Compared to R02 (new samples without reference NUMT exclusion), after excluding reference NUMTs, 16 Common NUMTs appeared in new samples, indicating these are **cohort-specific common insertion events** — occurring at high frequency in new samples of the current cohort but not recorded in public reference databases.

**Per-Sample Distribution**

7,360 samples (87.87%) had no novel NUMTs detected, while only 1,016 samples (12.13%) carried ≥1 novel NUMT. Per-sample mean of 0.19 NUMTs (SD = 1.09), range 0–39. This extremely low per-sample NUMT count suggests that novel NUMTs are exceedingly rare events, with the vast majority of individuals carrying no NUMT insertions beyond those in known databases.

**Chromosomal Distribution**

Novel NUMT events were sparsely distributed across chromosomes. The highest-count chromosomes were chr1 (235), chr13 (375), and chr5 (128). Compared to the chromosomal distribution pattern of the full sample set, the relative enrichment of chr13 was particularly notable (375/1,604 = 23.4%), suggesting chr13 may be a hotspot for novel NUMT insertions.

**Most Recurrent Novel NUMT Clusters**

Top recurrent novel NUMT clusters:
1. chr13:17231903–17232120 (frequency 3.87%, 324 samples) — Low-frequency
2. chr11:4634854–4635460 (frequency 3.64%, 305 samples) — Low-frequency
3. chr12:66057590–66057708 (frequency 3.33%, 279 samples) — Low-frequency

The highest frequency of these novel NUMTs was only 3.87%, far below the frequency of reference NUMTs (>40%), further confirming they are recently occurred insertion events.

**Statistical Evidence**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total distinct NUMT regions | 1,604 |
| Median NUMT length | 67 bp |
| Mean NUMT length | 74.6 bp |
| Per-sample NUMT mean ± SD | 0.19 ± 1.09 |
| Per-sample NUMT range | 0–39 |
| Zero-NUMT samples | 7,360 (87.87%) |
| NUMT-carrying samples | 1,016 (12.13%) |
| Total NUMT clusters | 1,098 |
| Ultra-rare cluster proportion | 87.98% (966/1,098) |

**Domain Interpretation**

The finding that only 12.13% of samples carry at least one novel NUMT has important implications: (1) the vast majority of NUMT insertion events have been captured by existing databases, and the "discovery window" for the human NUMT landscape is narrowing; (2) among the remaining 12% of carriers, NUMTs are predominantly Ultra-rare (87.98%), suggesting the rate of novel NUMT insertions is approximately 1 per 8 individuals; (3) the relative enrichment of novel NUMTs on chr13 may relate to local sequence features of this chromosome (e.g., LINE element density, chromatin accessibility).

The 16 cohort-specific Common NUMTs represent one of the most important findings of this study. These NUMTs occur at frequencies ≥0.05% in new samples (i.e., ≥4 carriers among 8,376 samples) but are not recorded in public reference databases. They may represent East Asian population-specific NUMT insertion events, warranting structural validation through long-read sequencing.

**Limitations**

1. Sequencing depth differences between new samples and reference databases may cause some known NUMTs to be missed and misclassified as "novel."
2. Incomplete coverage of reference NUMT databases may overestimate the number of "novel" NUMTs.
3. Short-read sequencing cannot accurately resolve complex NUMT structures (e.g., tandem repeats, mtDNA-mtDNA breakpoints).
4. The clinical and biological significance of novel NUMTs requires further functional validation.

## 讨论 (中文)

### 关键发现归纳

本研究通过严格阈值（≥5 discordant reads）的 NUMT 检测流程，在 8,376 例样本中系统描绘了人类 NUMT 的频率分布景观。核心发现包括：

1. **NUMT 景观的高度保守性**：85% 的 NUMT 事件与已知参考数据库重叠，提示人类 NUMT 景观的主体框架已被充分刻画。这一结果与 Wei et al. (2022) 在 100,000 Genomes Project 中的发现一致，进一步证实了 NUMT 插入的跨人群保守性。

2. **Ultra-rare NUMTs 的主导地位**：无论是否排除参考 NUMTs，Ultra-rare 类别始终占据最大比例（79–94%），反映了 NUMT 插入事件的持续发生和负向选择压力。这一发现支持 NUMT 作为"分子化石"记录线粒体-核基因组 DNA 转移历史的理论框架。

3. **队列特异的 Common NUMTs**：排除参考数据库后，本研究鉴定出 16 个队列特异的 Common NUMTs，最高频率达 3.87%。这些 NUMT 可能代表东亚人群特异的插入事件，为理解 NUMT 的人群分化提供了新线索。

4. **NUMT 长度与频率的反向关系**：全样本分析中 NUMT 中位长度为 111 bp，而新样本中仅为 67 bp，且不包含全基因组长度 NUMT。这一模式支持"大片段 NUMT 受负向选择清除"的假说。

### 与已有研究的比较

本研究鉴定的 NUMT 数量（111,487 distinct regions, 2,835 clusters）与 Wei et al. (2022) 报道的 254,195 NUMTs（1,637 distinct NUMTs, ≥5 discordant reads）在数量级上可比。本研究 cluster 数量更多（2,835 vs. 1,637），可能反映了：(1) 本研究纳入了更多东亚人群样本，捕获了更多人群特异的 NUMT；(2) 本研究样本涵盖了更广泛的公共数据库，增加了 NUMT 变异的检出范围。

### 机制推断

NUMT 插入的非随机染色体分布（chr1、chr2、chr11、chr17 富集；chr19、chr18 最低）提示插入过程可能受到局部序列特征的影响。可能的机制包括：(1) LINE-1 反转录转座子介导的靶向整合（Target-Primed Reverse Transcription, TPRT）；(2) 非同源末端连接（NHEJ）修复过程中的 mtDNA 片段捕获；(3) 染色质可及性调控的插入偏好。chr13 上新 NUMT 的相对富集值得进一步通过 permutation test 验证其统计学显著性。

### 局限性

1. **测序深度的批次效应**：公共参考数据与本队列新样本的测序深度存在差异，可能影响 NUMT 检测的灵敏度和特异性。
2. **参考数据库的人群偏倚**：现有 NUMT 参考数据库主要基于欧洲裔人群，可能导致东亚人群特异 NUMT 被误判为"新发现"。
3. **短读长测序的技术局限**：无法准确解析复杂 NUMT 结构（串联重复、融合 NUMT），且对重复区域的检测灵敏度有限。
4. **缺乏功能验证**：本研究仅为描述性分析，新发现 NUMT 的功能意义需要通过长读长测序、表观遗传学分析和转录组学数据进行验证。
5. **频率分类阈值的任意性**：Common/Low-frequency/Rare/Ultra-rare 的阈值设定基于先验经验，可能需要根据具体人群和研究目的进行调整。

### 后续研究方向

1. 通过 Oxford Nanopore 或 PacBio 长读长测序验证高频新发现 NUMT 的结构。
2. 将新发现 NUMT 与基因功能注释（如 GENCODE）重叠，评估其对基因表达的潜在影响。
3. 结合表型数据，探索 NUMT 负荷与疾病表型的关联。
4. 开展跨人群的 NUMT 比较分析，揭示 NUMT 插入的人群分化模式和时间轨迹。

## Discussion (EN)

### Key Findings Summary

Through a strict-threshold (≥5 discordant reads) NUMT detection pipeline, this study systematically characterized the frequency distribution landscape of human NUMTs across 8,376 samples. Core findings include:

1. **High conservation of the NUMT landscape**: 85% of NUMT events overlap with known reference databases, indicating that the main framework of the human NUMT landscape has been comprehensively characterized. This result is consistent with findings from Wei et al. (2022) in the 100,000 Genomes Project, further confirming the cross-population conservation of NUMT insertions.

2. **Dominance of Ultra-rare NUMTs**: Regardless of whether reference NUMTs were excluded, the Ultra-rare category consistently accounted for the largest proportion (79–94%), reflecting the ongoing occurrence of NUMT insertion events and negative selective pressure. This finding supports the theoretical framework of NUMTs as "molecular fossils" recording the history of mitochondrial-to-nuclear DNA transfer.

3. **Cohort-specific Common NUMTs**: After excluding reference databases, this study identified 16 cohort-specific Common NUMTs, with the highest frequency reaching 3.87%. These NUMTs may represent East Asian population-specific insertion events, providing new clues for understanding population differentiation of NUMTs.

4. **Inverse relationship between NUMT length and frequency**: The median NUMT length was 111 bp in the full sample analysis but only 67 bp in new samples, with no full-genome-length NUMTs detected. This pattern supports the hypothesis that large-fragment NUMTs are purged by negative selection.

### Comparison with Previous Studies

The number of NUMTs identified in this study (111,487 distinct regions, 2,835 clusters) is comparable in order of magnitude to the 254,195 NUMTs (1,637 distinct NUMTs, ≥5 discordant reads) reported by Wei et al. (2022). The higher cluster count in this study (2,835 vs. 1,637) may reflect: (1) the inclusion of more East Asian population samples, capturing more population-specific NUMTs; (2) the incorporation of a broader range of public databases, increasing the detection range of NUMT variants.

### Mechanistic Inference

The non-random chromosomal distribution of NUMT insertions (enrichment on chr1, chr2, chr11, chr17; lowest on chr19, chr18) suggests that the insertion process may be influenced by local sequence features. Possible mechanisms include: (1) LINE-1 retrotransposon-mediated targeted integration (Target-Primed Reverse Transcription, TPRT); (2) capture of mtDNA fragments during non-homologous end joining (NHEJ) repair; (3) chromatin accessibility-regulated insertion preference. The relative enrichment of novel NUMTs on chr13 warrants further statistical validation through permutation testing.

### Limitations

1. **Batch effects in sequencing depth**: Differences in sequencing depth between public reference data and newly recruited cohort samples may affect the sensitivity and specificity of NUMT detection.
2. **Population bias in reference databases**: Existing NUMT reference databases are primarily based on European-ancestry populations, potentially leading to misclassification of East Asian population-specific NUMTs as "novel."
3. **Technical limitations of short-read sequencing**: Inability to accurately resolve complex NUMT structures (tandem repeats, fusion NUMTs) and limited sensitivity for detecting NUMTs in repetitive regions.
4. **Lack of functional validation**: This study is descriptive in nature; the functional significance of novel NUMTs requires validation through long-read sequencing, epigenetic analysis, and transcriptomic data.
5. **Arbitrariness of frequency classification thresholds**: The Common/Low-frequency/Rare/Ultra-rare threshold settings are based on prior experience and may need adjustment according to specific populations and research objectives.

### Future Directions

1. Validate the structure of high-frequency novel NUMTs through Oxford Nanopore or PacBio long-read sequencing.
2. Overlap novel NUMTs with gene functional annotations (e.g., GENCODE) to assess their potential impact on gene expression.
3. Integrate phenotypic data to explore associations between NUMT burden and disease phenotypes.
4. Conduct cross-population NUMT comparative analyses to reveal population differentiation patterns and temporal trajectories of NUMT insertions.
