# SCI results | 结果描述

**English**

Across 35,020 NUMTs clusters, the distribution of cluster size was highly skewed (POS mean 2.43, median 1), indicating that most clusters are singletons while a small subset forms strong hotspots. The largest hotspot occurred on chr1:629,082–634,861 with POS=5,104, suggesting a pronounced aggregation of breakpoint positions in this locus.  

Sample recurrence tracked cluster size closely (Spearman r=0.976). The most recurrent cluster was chr17:22,519,353–22,532,169, shared by 4,723 samples, and several additional loci showed high recurrence (e.g., chr1:629,082–634,861 with 4,424 samples). These results support the presence of a limited number of genome-wide NUMTs hotspots that are widely shared across individuals.

**中文**

在 35,020 个 NUMTs 聚类中，聚类规模分布呈明显偏态（POS 均值 2.43，中位数 1），提示多数聚类为单例，而少数位点形成显著热点。最大的热点位于 chr1:629,082–634,861，POS=5,104，显示该区域断点高度聚集。  

样本复现度与聚类规模高度相关（Spearman r=0.976）。复现度最高的聚类位于 chr17:22,519,353–22,532,169，被 4,723 个样本共享；此外 chr1:629,082–634,861 等位点亦表现出高复现度（4,424 样本）。这些结果表明，NUMTs 在全基因组范围内存在少数但广泛共享的热点区域。
