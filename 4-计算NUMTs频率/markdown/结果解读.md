Results (English)

Across 36,376 distinct NUMTs, the length distribution was right-skewed, with a mean of 5,362.7 bp and a median of 4,788 bp (min 1 bp, max 16,510 bp; 95th percentile 12,932.8 bp). Short insertions were frequent: 3,608 events were <400 bp (9.92% of all distinct NUMTs), consistent with a long-tailed length spectrum dominated by mid-length fragments and a substantial short-length component.

Population frequency was dominated by ultra-rare events (34,354; 94.44%), while rare and common classes comprised 1,587 (4.36%) and 435 (1.20%) events, respectively (private merged into ultra-rare for the pie chart). At the sample level, all individuals carried at least one common NUMT (4,723/4,723; 100%), whereas 56.85% carried at least one rare NUMT, 29.24% carried at least one ultra-rare NUMT, and 46.03% carried at least one private NUMT, indicating widespread common hotspots alongside a large pool of low-frequency events.

结果（中文）

在 36,376 个 distinct NUMT 中，长度分布呈右偏，均值 5,362.7 bp，中位数 4,788 bp（最小 1 bp，最大 16,510 bp；95 分位 12,932.8 bp）。短片段占比不低：<400 bp 的事件有 3,608 个，占 9.92%，提示长度谱具有明显长尾，同时保留相当比例的短片段事件。

人群频率以 ultra-rare 为主（34,354；94.44%），rare 和 common 分别为 1,587（4.36%）与 435（1.20%）（饼图中 private 并入 ultra-rare）。在样本层面，所有个体均携带至少一个 common NUMT（4,723/4,723；100%），而携带至少一个 rare、ultra-rare、private NUMT 的比例分别为 56.85%、29.24% 和 46.03%，说明少数高频热点与大量低频事件共存。
