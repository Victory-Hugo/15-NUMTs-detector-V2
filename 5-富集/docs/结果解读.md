# NUMT 断点富集分析结果解读

---

## 方法与材料（中文）

### 研究对象与数据来源

本研究共纳入 1,245 个核基因组 NUMT（Nuclear Mitochondrial DNA Segments）断点（所有频率类合并）以及 970 个对应的线粒体 DNA（mtDNA）断点，依据 NUMT 在研究队列中的人群频率进行分层：common（n = 30）、low-frequency（n = 34）、rare（n = 132）及 ultra-rare（n = 1,049）。断点数据来源于严格阈值过滤后的高置信断点集，频率分层标准由频率聚类分析（`4-numt-frequency-by-cluster.tsv`）确定。参考基因组版本为 GRCh38（常染色体+X+Y 总长 3,088,269,832 bp）；mtDNA 参考序列长度 16,569 bp。

### 富集检验方法

富集检验采用基于置换的排列检验（permutation test）。对每个断点，以断点为中心向两侧各延伸 100 bp，形成 200 bp 的侧翼窗口（flank window），随后检验该窗口落入目标基因组区域（target region BED 文件）的比例是否显著高于（富集）或低于（耗尽）随机预期。

**置换策略：** 在参考基因组（或 mtDNA）范围内对断点坐标进行随机化，共运行 10,000 次置换（`simulation_runs = 10,000`），以随机分布的观测百分比作为零假设分布。随机种子固定为 20260407，每个任务派生确定性种子以保证可重现。

**统计指标：**

- `p_value_greater`（富集 p 值）：随机置换中观测百分比 ≥ 实际观测值的比例；p ≤ 0.05 判定为显著富集。
- `p_value_less`（耗尽 p 值）：随机置换中观测百分比 ≤ 实际观测值的比例；p ≤ 0.05 判定为显著耗尽。

未进行多重校正（本分析以名义显著性 p < 0.05 为阈值，解读时须注意假阳性风险）。

### 核基因组目标区域

共纳入 26 类核基因组功能/结构注释区域，包括：启动子（Promoter）、蛋白编码区（Protein coding region）、节段重复（Segmental duplications）、STR（短串联重复）、CpG 岛、微卫星、起始/终止密码子、snRNA/snoRNA/miRNA、功能元件、逆转录子（Retroposon）、CDS、SINE、DNA 转座子（rmsk-DNA）、着丝粒、卫星序列、LTR、外显子、UTR、LINE、简单重复序列、基因组超级复制（Genomic superdups）、基因、内含子及调控元件。

### mtDNA 目标区域

粗粒度分析：D-loop、蛋白编码区（Protein coding）、核糖体 RNA（rRNA）、转运 RNA（tRNA）共 4 类区域。

细粒度分析：涵盖 mtDNA 全部 37 个注释单元，包括 ATP6、ATP8、COX1–3、CYTB、ND1–6、ND4L 等 13 个蛋白编码基因，RNR1（12S rRNA）、RNR2（16S rRNA）两个 rRNA 基因，D-loop 的两个亚区（D-loop-A 和 D-loop-B），以及 22 个 tRNA 基因（TRNA、TRNC 等）。

### 软件与工具

- **bedtools**（区域重叠计算）
- **Python 3**（置换检验主逻辑）
- **R / Rscript**（热图与条形图可视化，`pheatmap` 等）
- 流水线脚本：`run_enrichment_analysis.sh`；配置文件：`Config.yaml`

## Methods and Materials (EN)

### Study cohort and data sources

A total of 1,245 nuclear NUMT breakpoints (all frequency strata combined) and 970 corresponding mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) breakpoints were included. NUMTs were stratified by population frequency into four classes: common (*n* = 30), low-frequency (*n* = 34), rare (*n* = 132), and ultra-rare (*n* = 1,049). Breakpoints were derived from a stringent-threshold, high-confidence breakpoint set; frequency strata were defined by cluster-level population frequency analysis (`4-numt-frequency-by-cluster.tsv`). The reference genome was GRCh38 (total autosomal + chrX + chrY length: 3,088,269,832 bp); the mtDNA reference was 16,569 bp.

### Enrichment testing

Enrichment was assessed using a permutation-based test. For each breakpoint, a 200 bp flanking window was constructed by extending 100 bp on each side. The observed proportion of windows overlapping each target region BED file was compared against a null distribution generated by 10,000 permutation rounds (`simulation_runs = 10,000`), in which breakpoint coordinates were randomised across the reference sequence. A fixed base seed of 20260407 was used, with task-specific deterministic seeds derived for reproducibility.

**Statistical metrics:**

- *p*_greater (enrichment *p*-value): proportion of permutations in which the random overlap percentage ≥ observed; *p* ≤ 0.05 denotes significant enrichment.
- *p*_less (depletion *p*-value): proportion of permutations in which the random overlap percentage ≤ observed; *p* ≤ 0.05 denotes significant depletion.

No multiple-testing correction was applied; results are reported at nominal *p* < 0.05, and the attendant false-positive risk is acknowledged in the Discussion.

### Nuclear genomic target regions

Twenty-six functional/structural annotation categories were evaluated, including promoters, protein-coding regions, segmental duplications, STRs, CpG islands, microsatellites, start/stop codons, snRNA/snoRNA/miRNA, functional elements, retroposons, CDS, SINEs, rmsk-DNA elements, centromeres, satellite sequences, LTRs, exons, UTRs, LINEs, simple repeats, genomic superduplications, genes, introns, and regulatory elements.

### mtDNA target regions

Coarse-grained analysis: four broad annotation classes—D-loop, protein-coding, ribosomal RNA (rRNA), and transfer RNA (tRNA).

Fine-grained analysis: all 37 annotated mtDNA units, comprising 13 protein-coding genes (ATP6, ATP8, COX1–3, CYTB, ND1–6, ND4L), two rRNA genes (RNR1/12S rRNA, RNR2/16S rRNA), two D-loop subregions (D-loop-A and D-loop-B), and 22 tRNA genes.

### Software

- **bedtools** (genomic intersection)
- **Python 3** (permutation test logic)
- **R / Rscript** (visualisation: heatmaps and barplots)
- Pipeline: `run_enrichment_analysis.sh`; configuration: `Config.yaml`

## 结果（中文）

### R01 — 核基因组 NUMT 断点的整体富集模式

对所有 NUMT 断点（n = 1,245）进行核基因组 26 类区域的富集检验，共检出 8 项显著关联（Fig. 1；Fig. 2；Table 1）。

**显著富集的区域：**

- **节段重复（Segmental duplications，自定义集）**：163/1,245 个断点（13.1%）位于侧翼窗口内，显著高于随机预期（*p* < 0.0001，置换检验）。
- **基因组超级复制（Genomic superdups）**：同样 163/1,245 个断点（13.1%；*p* < 0.0001）；与节段重复完全相同的计数提示两套注释文件存在高度重叠，建议后续分析合并或去冗余处理。
- **简单重复序列（Simple Repeats）**：197/1,245（15.8%；*p* < 0.0001）——为所有富集区域中绝对断点数量最多、富集显著性最强的类别之一。
- **外显子（Exon）**：128/1,245（10.3%；*p* < 0.0001）。
- **卫星序列（Satellite）**：50/1,245（4.0%；*p* = 0.0009）。

**显著耗尽的区域：**

- **CpG 岛**：仅 5/1,245（0.4%）；*p*（耗尽）= 0.036。
- **LINE（长散在核元件）**：314/1,245（25.2%）；尽管绝对数量较大，但显著低于随机预期（*p*（耗尽）= 0.009）。
- **内含子（Intron）**：632/1,245（50.8%）；同样低于随机预期（*p*（耗尽）= 0.013）。

**解读：** NUMT 断点倾向于聚集在基因组结构不稳定区域（节段重复、卫星序列、简单重复序列），这与 NUMT 插入依赖双链断裂修复（DSBR）的机制一致——上述区域本身重复结构复杂，可促进错误修复和错位重组。外显子区域的富集在直觉上令人意外；若未进行严格的选择压力校正，需谨慎解读，可能与 NUMT 断点在功能区域边界集中有关，亦可能受外显子注释范围定义影响。LINE 区域的耗尽则提示 NUMT 插入在富含 LINE 的异染色质区域受到约束，或 LINE 自身竞争性占据了可用的基因组空间。CpG 岛及内含子的耗尽暗示着转录活跃的调控区域对 NUMT 插入具有负选择效应。

**局限性：** 未进行多重校正（26 类区域 × 5 频率类 = 130 个检验），部分边界显著结果（如 CpG 岛 *p* = 0.036）可能为假阳性。节段重复与基因组超级复制注释的高度重叠需独立核实。

### R02 — 核基因组 NUMT 断点富集的频率依赖性差异

按频率分层后，节段重复/基因组超级复制及简单重复序列在所有频率层均保持显著富集（Table 2）；而若干区域仅在特定频率层显现显著性（Fig. 1；Fig. 2）。

**Common NUMTs（n = 30）：**

- 富集：节段重复（9/30 = 30.0%，*p* < 0.0001）、基因组超级复制（9/30 = 30.0%，*p* < 0.0001）、rmsk-DNA 转座子元件（6/30 = 20.0%，*p* = 0.010）、简单重复序列（6/30 = 20.0%，*p* = 0.040）。
- 耗尽：SINE（1/30 = 3.3%，*p* = 0.006）。

**Low-frequency NUMTs（n = 34）：**

- 富集：节段重复（6/34 = 17.6%，*p* = 0.009）、基因组超级复制（6/34 = 17.6%，*p* = 0.010）、着丝粒区域（4/34 = 11.8%，*p* = 0.004）、卫星序列（4/34 = 11.8%，*p* = 0.008）、简单重复序列（8/34 = 23.5%，*p* = 0.006）。

**Rare NUMTs（n = 132）：**

- 富集：节段重复（28/132 = 21.2%，*p* < 0.0001）、基因组超级复制（28/132 = 21.2%，*p* < 0.0001）、卫星序列（10/132 = 7.6%，*p* = 0.002）、简单重复序列（40/132 = 30.3%，*p* < 0.0001）。
- 耗尽：蛋白编码区（0/132 = 0%，*p* = 0.035）、CDS（0/132 = 0%，*p* = 0.035）、LTR（6/132 = 4.5%，*p* = 0.003）。

**Ultra-rare NUMTs（n = 1,049）：**

- 富集：节段重复（120/1,049 = 11.4%，*p* < 0.0001）、基因组超级复制（120/1,049 = 11.4%，*p* < 0.0001）、SINE（256/1,049 = 24.4%，*p* = 0.022）、外显子（109/1,049 = 10.4%，*p* = 0.0002）、简单重复序列（143/1,049 = 13.6%，*p* < 0.0001）。
- 耗尽：LINE（263/1,049 = 25.1%，*p* = 0.015）。

**解读：** 高频 NUMT（common/low-frequency）主要富集于节段重复与卫星附近，与这类区域促进经典 NHEJ 修复途径后整合的生物学机制一致。Low-frequency NUMTs 特异性富集于着丝粒区域，提示着丝粒附近的结构特性可能为 NUMT 低频保留提供条件。Rare NUMTs 中蛋白编码区/CDS 完全缺失断点，强烈支持功能性选择压力对 NUMT 插入的清除作用。Ultra-rare NUMTs 中 SINE 富集提示这类极低频插入事件可能利用 SINE 介导的同源重组机制。

### R03 — mtDNA 断点整体富集模式（粗粒度分析）

对所有 mtDNA 断点（n = 970）进行 D-loop、蛋白编码区、rRNA 及 tRNA 四类区域的富集检验（Table 3；Fig. 3；Fig. 4）。

**显著富集：**

- **D-loop**：109/970（11.2%），显著富集（*p* < 0.0001）——D-loop 约占 mtDNA 的 7%（~1,122/16,569 bp），而断点占比达 11.2%，提示超出预期的聚集。
- **rRNA 区域**：213/970（22.0%，*p* < 0.0001）——rRNA 区域约占 mtDNA 的 15%，断点占比显著更高。

**显著耗尽：**

- **蛋白编码区**：650/970（67.0%，*p*（耗尽）< 0.0001）——蛋白编码区约占 mtDNA 的 68%，但断点比例显著低于随机预期。
- **tRNA 区域**：190/970（19.6%，*p*（耗尽）= 0.0004）——tRNA 区域约占 mtDNA 的 9%，但断点占比达 19.6%，整体 tRNA 的耗尽信号在后文细粒度分析中将得到进一步细化（部分 tRNA 亚型实为富集）。

**分频率层分析：**

- D-loop 富集在 common（4/16 = 25%，*p* = 0.019）、rare（15/89 = 16.9%，*p* = 0.001）及 ultra-rare（87/844 = 10.3%，*p* = 0.0001）均显著；low-frequency 层不显著（*p* = 0.173）。
- 蛋白编码区耗尽主要体现在 low-frequency（12/21 = 57.1%，*p* = 0.038）、rare（54/89 = 60.7%，*p* = 0.0003）及 ultra-rare（575/844 = 68.1%，*p* < 0.0001）。
- rRNA 富集集中在 rare（23/89 = 25.8%，*p* = 0.022）及 ultra-rare（183/844 = 21.7%，*p* < 0.0001）。
- tRNA 耗尽主要体现在 low-frequency（0/21 = 0%，*p* = 0.003）及 ultra-rare（164/844 = 19.4%，*p* = 0.0004）。

**解读：** D-loop 作为 mtDNA 的非编码调控区，其三链 D-loop 结构及高度可变的 HVS（高变序列区）本身即为复制起始相关的 DNA 动态重构区域，可能因此成为 mtDNA 断裂的易发位点。rRNA 区域的富集可能源于其相对高的转录活性和核糖体相关暴露结构。蛋白编码区受到纯化选择保护，不同 mtDNA 片段的整合事件倾向于避开功能性编码序列，与进化上对 NUMT 来源片段的 mtDNA 功能保守性一致。

### R04 — mtDNA 断点细粒度富集模式（逐基因分析）

对 970 个 mtDNA 断点进行 mtDNA 全部 37 个注释单元的细粒度富集检验（Table 4；Fig. 5；Fig. 6）。

#### R04a — D-loop 亚区域

- **D-loop-A**（D-loop 5' 亚区）：66/970（6.8%）——全体断点（*p* < 0.0001）、rare（13/89 = 14.6%，*p* < 0.0001）及 ultra-rare（50/844 = 5.9%，*p* = 0.0003）均显著富集。D-loop-A 包含 H 链复制起始点（OH）及 HVS1，为 mtDNA 断裂热点的候选区域。
- **D-loop-B**（D-loop 3' 亚区）：43/970（4.4%）——全体（*p* = 0.036）和 common（3/16 = 18.8%，*p* = 0.017）显著富集，覆盖 HVS2 区域。

#### R04b — rRNA 基因

- **RNR1（12S rRNA）**：98/970（10.1%）——全体（*p* = 0.0001）、rare（15/89 = 16.9%，*p* = 0.001）及 ultra-rare（78/844 = 9.2%，*p* = 0.009）均富集。
- **RNR2（16S rRNA）**：124/970（12.8%）——全体（*p* = 0.021）及 ultra-rare（111/844 = 13.2%，*p* = 0.014）富集。

RNR2（16S rRNA）为迄今已知最常见的 NUMT 来源区域之一，本结果与文献一致。[Citation Needed: reference for rRNA-derived NUMTs]

#### R04c — 蛋白编码基因（耗尽）

以下 7 个蛋白编码基因在全体断点层面显著耗尽（均为 all 频率类，Table 4）：

| 基因 | 观测断点 | 百分比 | *p*（耗尽） |
|------|---------|--------|-----------|
| ATP6 | 33/970 | 3.4% | 0.0018 |
| ATP8 | 12/970 | 1.2% | 0.0032 |
| COX3 | 45/970 | 4.6% | 0.034 |
| CYTB | 64/970 | 6.6% | 0.034 |
| ND2 | 38/970 | 3.9% | <0.0001 |
| ND3 | 16/970 | 1.6% | 0.001 |
| ND4L | 16/970 | 1.6% | 0.004 |

ND2 的耗尽信号在 ultra-rare 层（33/844 = 3.9%，*p* < 0.0001）同样极为显著，提示这一规律在极低频插入事件中尤为稳健。

#### R04d — tRNA 基因（混合信号）

虽然整体 tRNA 区域（粗粒度）呈耗尽趋势，细粒度分析揭示 tRNA 内部存在方向性分化：

**耗尽的 tRNA（全体断点）：** TRNG（6/970 = 0.6%，*p* = 0.004）、TRNI（9/970 = 0.9%，*p* = 0.039）、TRNK（7/970 = 0.7%，*p* = 0.009）、TRNR（7/970 = 0.7%，*p* = 0.011）、TRNT（9/970 = 0.9%，*p* = 0.043）、TRNW（7/970 = 0.7%，*p* = 0.008）。

**富集的 tRNA（全体断点）：** TRNC（24/970 = 2.5%，*p* = 0.025）、TRNF（25/970 = 2.6%，*p* = 0.017）、TRNY（26/970 = 2.7%，*p* = 0.006）。

**解读：** 6 个 tRNA 的显著耗尽与其功能上的不可或缺性一致——mtDNA 编码的 22 个 tRNA 对线粒体蛋白质翻译至关重要，其断裂将严重损害线粒体功能，从而受到强烈负选择。TRNC、TRNF、TRNY 的富集则提示这些特定 tRNA 基因附近可能存在利于 mtDNA 断裂的序列或结构特征（如邻近转录终止信号、特殊二级结构等），值得进一步机制探索。

## Results (EN)

### R01 — Overall nuclear genomic enrichment of NUMT breakpoints

Permutation-based enrichment testing of all NUMT breakpoints (*n* = 1,245) across 26 nuclear annotation categories identified 8 significant associations (Fig. 1; Fig. 2; Table 1).

**Significantly enriched regions:**

- **Segmental duplications (custom)**: 163 of 1,245 breakpoints (13.1%) fell within flanking windows, significantly exceeding the random expectation (*p* < 0.0001, permutation test).
- **Genomic superduplications**: 163 of 1,245 breakpoints (13.1%; *p* < 0.0001). The identical count to segmental duplications suggests high overlap between the two annotation files; consolidation is recommended for downstream analyses.
- **Simple repeats**: 197 of 1,245 breakpoints (15.8%; *p* < 0.0001)—the category with the largest absolute enrichment.
- **Exons**: 128 of 1,245 breakpoints (10.3%; *p* < 0.0001).
- **Satellite sequences**: 50 of 1,245 breakpoints (4.0%; *p* = 0.0009).

**Significantly depleted regions:**

- **CpG islands**: only 5 of 1,245 breakpoints (0.4%); *p*_less = 0.036.
- **LINEs**: 314 of 1,245 breakpoints (25.2%); significantly below random expectation (*p*_less = 0.009).
- **Introns**: 632 of 1,245 breakpoints (50.8%); below random expectation (*p*_less = 0.013).

Enrichment of NUMT breakpoints in segmental duplications and simple repeats is consistent with integration via error-prone double-strand break repair (DSBR) in structurally complex genomic regions. Depletion at CpG islands and introns suggests negative selection against insertions disrupting regulatory or genic environments.

### R02 — Frequency-stratified nuclear enrichment patterns

Stratification by population frequency revealed category-specific enrichment profiles (Table 2; Fig. 1; Fig. 2). Segmental duplications/genomic superduplications and simple repeats were consistently enriched across all frequency classes. Several categories showed frequency-specific significance.

**Common NUMTs (*n* = 30):** Segmental duplications (9/30 = 30.0%, *p* < 0.0001), genomic superdups (9/30 = 30.0%, *p* < 0.0001), rmsk-DNA elements (6/30 = 20.0%, *p* = 0.010), and simple repeats (6/30 = 20.0%, *p* = 0.040) were enriched; SINEs were depleted (1/30 = 3.3%, *p* = 0.006).

**Low-frequency NUMTs (*n* = 34):** Enriched in segmental duplications (6/34 = 17.6%, *p* = 0.009), genomic superdups (6/34 = 17.6%, *p* = 0.010), centromeres (4/34 = 11.8%, *p* = 0.004), satellite sequences (4/34 = 11.8%, *p* = 0.008), and simple repeats (8/34 = 23.5%, *p* = 0.006).

**Rare NUMTs (*n* = 132):** Enriched in segmental duplications (28/132 = 21.2%, *p* < 0.0001), genomic superdups (28/132 = 21.2%, *p* < 0.0001), satellite sequences (10/132 = 7.6%, *p* = 0.002), and simple repeats (40/132 = 30.3%, *p* < 0.0001). Depleted in protein-coding regions (0/132 = 0%, *p* = 0.035), CDS (0/132 = 0%, *p* = 0.035), and LTR elements (6/132 = 4.5%, *p* = 0.003).

**Ultra-rare NUMTs (*n* = 1,049):** Enriched in segmental duplications (120/1,049 = 11.4%, *p* < 0.0001), genomic superdups (120/1,049 = 11.4%, *p* < 0.0001), SINEs (256/1,049 = 24.4%, *p* = 0.022), exons (109/1,049 = 10.4%, *p* = 0.0002), and simple repeats (143/1,049 = 13.6%, *p* < 0.0001). Depleted in LINEs (263/1,049 = 25.1%, *p* = 0.015).

The complete absence of rare NUMT breakpoints in protein-coding and CDS regions is consistent with purifying selection against deleterious insertions in functional sequences.

### R03 — Broad-scale mtDNA enrichment analysis

Permutation testing across four broad mtDNA annotation classes revealed significant enrichment in the D-loop and rRNA regions and depletion in protein-coding and tRNA regions (Table 3; Fig. 3; Fig. 4).

**Enrichment:** The D-loop harboured 109 of 970 breakpoints (11.2%; *p* < 0.0001), exceeding its proportional genome representation (~6.8% of mtDNA). The rRNA region harboured 213 of 970 breakpoints (22.0%; *p* < 0.0001), disproportionately high relative to its mtDNA coverage (~15%).

**Depletion:** The protein-coding region contained 650 of 970 breakpoints (67.0%; *p*_less < 0.0001)—below random expectation despite covering ~68% of mtDNA. The tRNA region harboured 190 of 970 breakpoints (19.6%; *p*_less = 0.0004); however, this overall depletion signal reflects heterogeneity among individual tRNA genes (see R04d).

D-loop enrichment was significant in common (*p* = 0.019), rare (*p* = 0.001), and ultra-rare (*p* = 0.0001) strata. Protein-coding depletion was most prominent in low-frequency (*p* = 0.038), rare (*p* = 0.0003), and ultra-rare (*p* < 0.0001) strata.

### R04 — Fine-grained mtDNA enrichment analysis

Fine-grained permutation testing across all 37 annotated mtDNA units (all-frequency stratum, *n* = 970) identified 20 significant associations (Table 4; Fig. 5; Fig. 6).

**D-loop subregions:** D-loop-A was strongly enriched (66/970 = 6.8%; *p* < 0.0001), with consistent significance in rare (13/89 = 14.6%, *p* < 0.0001) and ultra-rare (50/844 = 5.9%, *p* = 0.0003) strata. D-loop-B was also enriched (43/970 = 4.4%; *p* = 0.036), particularly in common NUMTs (3/16 = 18.8%, *p* = 0.017).

**rRNA genes:** RNR1 (12S rRNA) was enriched (98/970 = 10.1%; *p* = 0.0001), as was RNR2 (16S rRNA; 124/970 = 12.8%; *p* = 0.021). Both signals were robust in the ultra-rare stratum (RNR1: 78/844 = 9.2%, *p* = 0.009; RNR2: 111/844 = 13.2%, *p* = 0.014).

**Protein-coding genes (depleted):** Seven genes showed significant depletion across all breakpoints: *ATP6* (*p* = 0.0018), *ATP8* (*p* = 0.0032), *COX3* (*p* = 0.034), *CYTB* (*p* = 0.034), *ND2* (*p* < 0.0001), *ND3* (*p* = 0.001), and *ND4L* (*p* = 0.004) (Table 4). *ND2* depletion was particularly robust across the ultra-rare stratum (*p* < 0.0001).

**tRNA genes (divergent signals):** Among individual tRNA genes, six were significantly depleted: *TRNG* (*p* = 0.004), *TRNI* (*p* = 0.039), *TRNK* (*p* = 0.009), *TRNR* (*p* = 0.011), *TRNT* (*p* = 0.043), and *TRNW* (*p* = 0.008). Conversely, three tRNA genes showed significant enrichment: *TRNC* (24/970 = 2.5%, *p* = 0.025), *TRNF* (25/970 = 2.6%, *p* = 0.017), and *TRNY* (26/970 = 2.7%, *p* = 0.006) (Table 4).

## 讨论（中文）

### 核基因组背景：重复元件与结构不稳定区域驱动 NUMT 断点聚集

本研究最为一致的核基因组发现是：无论频率分层如何，NUMT 断点均显著富集于节段重复区域和简单重复序列。这与经典的 NUMT 整合机制高度契合——核基因组的 NUMT 整合普遍被认为通过双链断裂修复（DSBR）途径实现，而节段重复与简单重复区域因其富含同源序列，天然倾向于诱发链间错配修复（MMBIR）或非等位同源重组（NAHR），从而形成双链断裂"热点"。[Citation Needed]

值得注意的是，着丝粒区域富集仅出现在 low-frequency NUMT 中，提示中等频率的插入事件可能特异性地集中于着丝粒附近，这与着丝粒在细胞分裂期间存在周期性 DNA 修复需求的观点一致，但样本量（n = 34）较小，此发现需扩大队列验证。

Rare NUMT 中蛋白编码区和 CDS 的完全缺失（0/132）是本研究最强烈的频率依赖性信号之一，支持强负选择对编码区 NUMT 的清除作用。与此形成对比的是，ultra-rare NUMT 中外显子区域仍有一定富集（10.4%），这可能反映极低频 NUMT 尚未被充分清除、处于选择效应早期阶段，也可能部分由检测敏感性差异或样本代表性偏倚所致。

LINE 元件在全体和 ultra-rare NUMT 中的耗尽值得关注。LINE 主要分布于基因组的基因稀疏区和晚复制域，这些区域的异染色质状态可能抑制 NUMT 整合，或 LINE 自身的转座竞争性地消耗了 DSBR 的基因组可及性。无论何种机制，NUMT 与 LINE 的空间排斥均提示两类移动遗传元件在基因组中占据相对独立的生态位。

### mtDNA 背景：D-loop 和 rRNA 为断点热点，蛋白编码区受保护

D-loop 的显著超富集（11.2% vs. ~6.8% 基因组比例）跨越多个频率层，说明这一区域是跨频率类别的稳定 mtDNA 断裂热点。D-loop 的三链 DNA 结构、高变序列区（HVS1、HVS2）的高 GC 偏斜和高突变率，以及作为复制起始点（OH）的特殊功能状态，均可能增大局部 DNA 脆性。细粒度分析进一步揭示 D-loop 富集主要来源于 D-loop-A 亚区（含 HVS1/OH），D-loop-B 亚区（含 HVS2）的富集强度略弱，提示 5' 亚区为更主要的断裂起始位点。

rRNA 区域（RNR1 和 RNR2）的富集可能反映：（1）rRNA 基因的高转录活性导致 R-loop 形成和 DNA 拓扑应力积聚；（2）rRNA 序列是已知最早被鉴定的 NUMT 来源，其基因组整合频率本身较高，与本结果互为佐证。[Citation Needed]

蛋白编码区（尤其 ND2、ATP6、ATP8、ND3、ND4L）的显著耗尽，结合整体蛋白编码区耗尽的高度显著性（*p* < 0.0001），提供了迄今最直接的证据之一：mtDNA 蛋白编码基因的完整性被进化所保护，NUMT 中来自这些区域的 mtDNA 片段频率显著低于预期，可能反映：（a）这些基因所在 mtDNA 区段发生断裂后线粒体功能受损更为严重；（b）含有功能蛋白编码序列的 NUMT 片段在核基因组中产生有害作用而被清除。两种机制并非互斥。

tRNA 基因的二元分化模式（TRNG、TRNI、TRNK、TRNR、TRNT、TRNW 耗尽；TRNC、TRNF、TRNY 富集）是一个值得深入研究的新颖发现。耗尽的 tRNA 基因可能因其位置（嵌入蛋白编码基因之间）和功能重要性受到额外选择约束；而富集的 TRNC、TRNF、TRNY 则可能位于 mtDNA 基因组的易断裂结构域，或其序列特征使该区域相对于周围区域更易被纳入整合片段。这一发现需要结合 mtDNA 三维结构及线粒体核糖体相互作用数据进一步验证。

### 局限性

1. **多重检验未校正**：本分析涵盖核基因组 26 × 5 = 130 个检验、mtDNA 4 × 5 = 20 个粗粒度检验及 37 × 5 = 185 个细粒度检验，未进行 Bonferroni 或 FDR 校正。部分边界显著结果（如 *p* ≈ 0.03–0.04）可能为假阳性。建议后续报告经 FDR 校正后的显著性列表。
2. **样本量不均**：common（n = 30）和 low-frequency（n = 34）层样本量偏小，统计功效受限，部分显著结果（如着丝粒富集、D-loop-B 在 common 层的富集）置信区间宽，需扩大样本验证。
3. **区域注释重叠**：节段重复（自定义）与基因组超级复制注释产生完全相同的统计结果，表明存在冗余；若两套注释实质相同，则独立显著性声明不应成立。
4. **causal 方向性**：本研究为富集/耗尽的关联分析，不能区分 NUMT 断点的"插入偏好"（mtDNA 偏好在特定区域断裂后进入核基因组）与"选择后保留"（随机插入后某些区域的 NUMT 被正/负选择保留）。

## Discussion (EN)

### Nuclear genomic context: repeat elements and structural instability drive breakpoint clustering

The most consistent nuclear genomic finding across all frequency strata was significant enrichment of NUMT breakpoints within segmental duplications and simple repeat sequences. This is mechanistically consistent with integration via double-strand break repair (DSBR): segmental duplications and simple repeats are prone to ectopic recombination and microhomology-mediated replication slippage, generating the double-strand breaks that are prerequisite for NUMT integration. [Citation Needed]

Centromere enrichment restricted to low-frequency NUMTs (*n* = 34, *p* = 0.004) may reflect periodic DNA repair activity in centromeric regions during cell division, though the limited sample size precludes firm conclusions. The complete absence of protein-coding/CDS breakpoints among rare NUMTs (0/132) constitutes one of the strongest frequency-dependent signals identified and strongly supports purifying selection against insertions disrupting coding sequences. The residual exon enrichment in ultra-rare NUMTs (10.4%) may reflect pre-selection events yet to be purged, or may partly arise from detection bias.

LINE depletion across all and ultra-rare NUMTs suggests spatial exclusion between NUMT insertion sites and LINE-dense heterochromatic regions, consistent with the hypothesis that the two classes of repetitive elements occupy distinct genomic niches.

### mtDNA context: D-loop and rRNA as fragility hotspots; protein-coding regions are protected

Consistent D-loop enrichment across multiple frequency strata (11.2%, *p* < 0.0001) identifies this region as the most robust mtDNA fragility hotspot in our dataset. The triple-stranded D-loop structure, high-variability hypervariable segments (HVS1 and HVS2), and the presence of the heavy-strand replication origin (O_H) collectively predispose this region to strand breaks. Fine-grained analysis localises the dominant enrichment signal to D-loop-A (containing HVS1/O_H), with D-loop-B (HVS2) showing a weaker but significant secondary enrichment.

rRNA enrichment (RNR1 and RNR2 both enriched, *p* < 0.05) may reflect R-loop formation associated with high rRNA transcription rates and/or the well-documented origin of many NUMTs from rRNA-encoding regions. [Citation Needed]

Depletion of seven protein-coding genes—including *ND2*, *ATP6*, *ATP8*, *ND3*, and *ND4L*—with the strongest signals at *ND2* (*p* < 0.0001), provides direct evidence that mtDNA protein-coding integrity is evolutionarily protected. Whether this reflects preferential avoidance of fragility in these loci, more severe mitochondrial dysfunction from breaks within these genes, or nuclear-level purifying selection against protein-coding NUMT fragments remains to be determined.

The bifurcated tRNA enrichment/depletion pattern—six tRNAs depleted and three (TRNC, TRNF, TRNY) enriched—is a novel finding that warrants structural and functional investigation. Depleted tRNA genes may be constrained by their functional indispensability and genomic context flanked by protein-coding genes; enriched tRNA genes may reside in locally fragile mtDNA domains or harbour sequence features that facilitate capture into integrated fragments.

### Limitations

1. **Uncorrected multiple testing**: 130 nuclear, 20 coarse-grained mtDNA, and 185 fine-grained mtDNA tests were performed without FDR correction; borderline results (*p* ≈ 0.03–0.04) may be false positives. FDR-corrected significance thresholds should be reported in the final manuscript.
2. **Unequal stratum sizes**: common (*n* = 30) and low-frequency (*n* = 34) strata have limited statistical power; findings restricted to these strata require independent replication in larger cohorts.
3. **Annotation redundancy**: The identical statistics for segmental duplications and genomic superduplications indicate overlapping annotation files; these should be consolidated or differentiated before final reporting.
4. **Directionality**: Enrichment/depletion patterns reflect association, not causation. Insertion preference and post-insertion selection cannot be disentangled with the current analysis design.
